## Algorithm

1 Start the program.

2 Input the number of electricity substations (vertices).

3 Input the adjacency matrix representing the power lines and their weights.

4 Select the first substation as the starting vertex.

5 Mark the starting vertex as visited.

6 Find the minimum-weight edge connecting a visited vertex to an unvisited vertex.

7 Select that edge and mark the new vertex as visited.

8 Repeat steps 6 and 7 until all vertices are connected or \(V-1\) edges are selected.

9 Display the selected power lines and calculate their total cost.



Stop the program.
